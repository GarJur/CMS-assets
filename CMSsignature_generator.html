CMS Email Signature Generator

Fill in the fields, copy the signature preview, and paste it into Outlook or Gmail.

Preview

How to use:
1. Fill in the employee information.
2. Click “Update Signature.”
3. Click “Copy Signature.”
4. Paste it into Outlook, Gmail, or Apple Mail signature settings.

If copying does not preserve the layout, select the preview manually, copy it, and paste it into the email signature field.
